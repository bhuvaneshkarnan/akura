Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions By Families

Clear, honest answers to help you make an informed and timely decision for your loved one’s recovery.

How long does the rehabilitation program last at Akrura Vadipatti?

Our clinical programs typically range from 30 days to 90 days depending on the patient's substance history, physical condition, and dual-diagnosis psychiatric evaluation. A custom medical treatment timeline is determined after initial intake by our Chief Psychiatrist.

Can family members visit or communicate during treatment?

Yes. Following the initial stabilization and detoxification phase (typically 7–10 days), weekly telephonic updates are provided by counselors. Structured family counseling sessions and supervised campus visits are scheduled to rebuild trust and prepare for homecoming.

How do you protect patient privacy and confidentiality?

We maintain strict 100% medical confidentiality. Inpatient records are confidential, admissions are conducted discreetly, and no patient information or identities are ever disclosed publicly. Your loved one's dignity is protected at all times.

Do you assist with patient pickup or transport across Tamil Nadu?

Yes, we operate a 24/7 emergency medical assistance team with ambulance support. If an individual in Chennai, Coimbatore, Salem, Erode, Tiruppur, or Madurai needs immediate, safe transfer to our Vadipatti sanctuary, our trained team assists with respectful and safe coordination.

What happens after discharge? How do you prevent relapse?

Recovery is lifelong. Every patient receives a tailored Relapse Prevention Blueprint before discharge, accompanied by scheduled outpatient counseling check-ins, telephone helpline access, family check-ups, and participation in our alumni recovery network.
