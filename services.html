Specialized Inpatient Program

Alcohol De-Addiction & Detoxification

Chronic alcohol dependence requires meticulous medical oversight. Sudden cessation can lead to severe withdrawal complications like Delirium Tremens (DTs), tremors, and seizures. Our doctors provide safe withdrawal stabilization, vital organ monitoring, and cognitive therapy to break the craving cycle.

  • 24/7 Vital Signs & ICU Stabilization Protocols
  • Dual-Diagnosis Screening for Co-Occurring Anxiety
  • Liver Function & Nutritional Rebalancing
  • Craving Management & Anti-Craving Medications

Substance Abuse Recovery

Drug Abuse Rehabilitation & Renewal

Comprehensive clinical rehabilitation for chemical dependencies including opioids, brown sugar, cannabis/ganja, prescription sedatives, sleeping pills, and synthetic drugs. We treat the physical addiction and the psychological root causes with equal medical precision.

  • Non-Punitive, Dignity-Focused Environment
  • Targeted Neurochemical Detox Protocols
  • One-on-One CBT & Trauma Psychotherapy
  • Peer Group Dynamics & 12-Step Philosophy
Confidential Drug Consultation
Dual-Diagnosis & Mental Health

Psychiatric Care & Clinical Counseling

Over 60% of individuals struggling with substance dependence also battle underlying depression, anxiety disorders, bipolar condition, or unprocessed trauma. Our board-certified psychiatrists diagnose and treat both conditions simultaneously for lasting, true recovery.

  • Full Neuropsychiatric Diagnostic Assessments
  • Medication Management by Senior Psychiatrists
  • Stress Inoculation & Emotional Regulation
  • Structured Daily Routine & Sleep Restoration

Family Healing & Reconciliation

Family Counseling & Codependency Healing

Addiction is a family condition. Spouses, parents, and children endure tremendous emotional distress. Our clinical family therapists facilitate guided dialogue, resolve grief and resentment, and educate families on how to support long-term recovery without enabling relapse.

  • Weekly Counselor Telephonic Reports
  • Guided Family Reconciliation Sessions
  • Boundary Setting & Relapse Prevention Training
  • Post-Discharge Home Care Protocols
