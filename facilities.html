Vadipatti Oasis

A Peaceful Sanctuary For Renewal

Take a tour of our peaceful residential recovery campus in Vadipatti, Madurai. Designed to provide a noise-free, dignified, and clinically equipped healing environment.

Sanctuary Grounds

Lush Nature & Serene Walking Grounds

Surrounded by green coconut groves, fresh breeze, and open blue skies away from urban pollution, fostering tranquility and mental clarity.

Mindfulness Hall

Holistic Yoga & Pranayama Hall

Spacious, tranquil hall for daily morning mindfulness, pranayama breathwork, brain gym neuroplasticity drills, and guided relaxation.

Therapy Suites

Private Consultation & Therapy Suites

Discreet, sound-insulated counseling rooms where patients and families consult one-on-one with psychiatrists and clinical psychologists in total confidence.

Dining & Recreation

Nutritious Dining & Community Recreation

Clean, hygienic dining serving wholesome South Indian vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals, library, indoor games, and community recovery meetings.
