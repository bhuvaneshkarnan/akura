Vadipatti Campus

Campus Entrance & Residential Center

Authentic, peaceful sanctuary situated in Vadipatti, Madurai featuring secure campus grounds, 24/7 doctor supervision, and dedicated clinic patient transport.

Community Hall

Inpatient Recovery & Assembly Hall

Spacious community hall for daily morning mindfulness, pranayama breathwork, peer recovery circles, and transformative group psychotherapy.

Therapy Suites

Private 1-on-1 Psychotherapy Suites

Discreet, sound-insulated counseling cabins where clinical psychologists conduct personalized cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and trauma recovery in total privacy.

Family Suite

Family Guidance & Consultation Suite

Comfortable clinical consultation suite where doctors guide families through structured reconciliation, boundary restoration, and post-discharge home care.

Medical Clinic

Clinical Intake & Medical Evaluation Cabin

Dedicated clinical room for comprehensive medical evaluations, vitals tracking, safe detoxification protocols, and regular doctor examinations.

Dining & Recreation

Nutritious Dining & Wholesome Recreation

Clean, hygienic dining serving wholesome South Indian vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals, accompanied by library, indoor games, and community recovery meetings.
